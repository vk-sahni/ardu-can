# Fiverr gig: Custom KiCad PCB and embedded hardware design

## Gig title

**I will design custom PCBs, KiCad schematics and CAN bus hardware**

## Gig description

Bring your electronics idea from concept to a clear, well-documented PCB
design. I provide custom schematic capture and PCB layout in KiCad, with
component choices and deliverables tailored to your product, mechanical
constraints, and manufacturing plan.

### What I can help you build

- A new schematic from your requirements, block diagram, or reference design
- A routed PCB layout with component placement suited to your enclosure and
  interfaces
- Embedded hardware using your preferred MCU or main IC
- CAN-bus and other wired-interface hardware where appropriate
- Component selection, footprint creation, and library verification
- Design-for-manufacturing review against the fabrication rules you provide
- A 3D PCB preview and STEP export when the selected parts and models support it

### Your project workflow

1. **Define the design:** We review functionality, interfaces, power, board
   dimensions, components, and manufacturing requirements.
2. **Agree the scope:** You approve the deliverables, package, schedule,
   revision allowance, and any optional work before design begins.
3. **Develop the design:** I create or update the schematic, place and route
   the PCB, and review it against the agreed electrical and mechanical
   constraints.
4. **Deliver your files:** Receive the editable KiCad source and the exports
   included in your selected package.

### Portfolio example

My Ardu-CAN portfolio design showcases an STM32F412 MCU, TJA1051 CAN
transceiver, ICM-42688-P inertial sensor, power-protection and regulation
circuitry, a custom KiCad PCB, and an assembly STEP export. It demonstrates my
design workflow and documentation; the repository's engineering review
identifies the validation work still needed before fabrication.

### Project scope

Each order is agreed around your specific requirements. Gerber/drill outputs,
a sourced BOM, firmware, fabrication, assembly, and physical testing can be
discussed as separate scope items; they are included only when explicitly
listed in your package or custom offer. Design review helps prepare files for
your chosen supplier, while final acceptance and prototype validation depend
on that supplier and your product requirements.

For high-speed, RF, high-voltage, safety-critical, rigid-flex, or mechanically
complex designs, please message me first so I can review the requirements and
provide a realistic custom quote.

## Client requirements

To get started, please share:

1. What should the PCB do, and where will it be used?
2. Do you have a preferred MCU or main IC? Please include the exact part
   number if known.
3. Which interfaces, protocols, connectors, and pinouts do you need?
4. What input/output voltage, current, and power levels are required?
5. What are your board size, shape, mounting, and enclosure constraints?
6. Do you have preferred components, an existing schematic, block diagram, or
   reference design?
7. Which manufacturer or assembly service will you use, and do you have its
   design rules?
8. Which files do you need: KiCad source, manufacturing files, BOM, 3D STEP,
   firmware, or test support?
9. What is your target date, and how should design reviews and revisions work?
